# Chess Coach

A Mac app that teaches you chess. Play a coach that stops you on mistakes and explains them, turn your own games into puzzles, and learn openings by playing them on the board. Everything runs on your Mac.

This repo only holds the downloads.

**[Download Chess Coach](https://github.com/JamesMDominguez/chess-coach-releases/releases/latest/download/Chess.Coach.dmg)** (Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later)

## Install

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Chess Coach** to **Applications**.
2. Open it. macOS blocks it the first time, because this copy isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to Security, click **Open Anyway** next to Chess Coach, and confirm.

You only do this once.
